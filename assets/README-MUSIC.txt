PROMPT SUNO UTILISÉ / CONSERVÉ COMME RÉFÉRENCE
Instrumental only. Energetic, chic cosmopolitan TV-title-sequence music with a playful late-1990s / early-2000s urban feel and a light exotic travel flavour. Around 112 BPM. Punchy upright bass, crisp drums, bongos and congas, shaker, bright piano, vibraphone, cheeky brass and trumpet stabs, muted guitar, subtle lounge/exotica touches. Flirty, witty, sophisticated, colourful, confident, fast-moving and stylish — like a glamorous city montage before a trip. Strong hook in the first 3 seconds, rhythmic and memorable but not intrusive under spoken English. No vocals, no lyrics, no EDM drop, no heavy rock, no sentimental orchestral swell, no cartoon feel. Do not quote, recreate or closely imitate any existing melody. Aim for an original, polished, loop-friendly instrumental with a clean ending that can cycle smoothly.
